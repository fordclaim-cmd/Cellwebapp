Privacy Policy

Goliath Automated Bookkeeper

Effective date: June 11, 2026  ·  Operator: Goliath Solutions  ·  Contact: info@floridasbestpools.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the Goliath Automated Bookkeeper (the "Application") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when it connects to a QuickBooks Online ("QBO") company and the associated financial data sources. The Application is an internal bookkeeping automation tool. It is operated by Goliath Solutions ("Goliath", "we", "us") and is currently used to maintain the books of Florida's Best Pools LLC. It is not a consumer product and does not collect information from the general public.

1. Information we access

When you connect the Application to your QuickBooks Online company and the other data sources listed below, the Application accesses and processes business financial records, including:

  • QuickBooks Online data — chart of accounts, vendors, customers, bills, invoices, bank and credit-card transactions, and the company realm ID, via the Intuit QuickBooks Online API.
  • Bank and credit-card transactions — retrieved from your financial institution through Plaid.
  • Vendor invoices — structured invoice data from the Pool360 (POOLCORP) API and, where enabled, invoice emails from a designated business mailbox.
  • Bill-pay and accounts-receivable records — from Melio (AP) and Jobber (AR).

The Application processes business financial data only. It does not knowingly collect Social Security numbers, consumer financial accounts of the general public, health information, or other special-category personal data.

2. How we use the information

We use the information solely to perform automated bookkeeping for the connected company:

  • Classifying transactions against the chart of accounts.
  • Creating and reconciling bills, invoices, and payments in QuickBooks Online.
  • Matching bank movements to recorded bills and payouts to prevent double-counting.
  • Maintaining an audit trail of every record ingested and every write to QBO.

We do not sell or rent any data. We do not use QuickBooks data for advertising. We do not share QuickBooks data with any party except the service providers required to operate the Application (Section 4).

3. How we store and secure the information

  • Raw events and derived records are stored in a managed PostgreSQL database (Neon) and processed by a worker hosted on Railway.
  • All API credentials and OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted environment secrets and database fields, never in source control.
  • All data in transit is protected with TLS/HTTPS.
  • Access to the staging database and credentials is limited to authorized operators of Goliath.

4. Service providers (subprocessors)

The Application relies on the following third-party services to function. Each receives only the data necessary for its role and is bound by its own terms and privacy policy:

5. Data retention and deletion

We retain ingested records and the QuickBooks audit trail for as long as needed to maintain accurate books and to satisfy tax and accounting recordkeeping obligations. You may disconnect the Application from QuickBooks Online at any time (Section 6). On a verified deletion request, we will delete the staging data we hold for the connected company, except records we are required to retain by law.

6. Disconnecting

You can revoke the Application's access at any time from within QuickBooks Online (Apps → Disconnect) or via the Application's disconnect page. After disconnection, the Application stops accessing your QuickBooks data, and the stored OAuth tokens for that company are invalidated.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective date" above and posting the revised policy at this URL.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or about data handling can be directed to info@floridasbestpools.com.

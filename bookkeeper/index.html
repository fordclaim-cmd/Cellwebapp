Product

The Automated Bookkeeper

Every invoice, every bank movement, every bill — fed into QuickBooks Online automatically, classified and reconciled by AI. The work a bookkeeper used to do, running on a timer.

How it works

QuickBooks Online as the
single source of truth.

Four data pipelines flow into one ledger. The golden rule: invoices accrue, the bank feed clears — never both for the same dollar.

01

Ingest

Vendor invoices (Pool360, email), bank & card activity (Plaid), AR (Jobber), AP (Melio) land in a staging database.

02

Classify

Claude categorizes each transaction against the chart of accounts, learning from prior approvals.

03

Reconcile

Bank movements match to bills; payouts match to payments. Clearing accounts prevent double entries.

04

Post

Validated entries push to QuickBooks Online idempotently, with a full audit trail of every write.

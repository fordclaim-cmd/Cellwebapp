Disconnect

Remove the Automated Bookkeeper's access to your QuickBooks Online company.

From QuickBooks Online

You can revoke access at any time directly in QuickBooks:

  1. Sign in to QuickBooks Online.
  2. Go to Settings → Apps (or Apps → My Apps).
  3. Find Goliath Automated Bookkeeper and choose Disconnect.

Once disconnected, the Application immediately stops accessing your QuickBooks data and the stored OAuth tokens for your company are invalidated.

Data deletion

To request deletion of the staging data we hold for your company, email info@floridasbestpools.com. We will delete it except for records we are required to retain by law. See our Privacy Policy for details.

About

Big tech energy.
Small-business heart.

We started Goliath Solutions because the companies that keep this country running — the roofers, the pool techs, the adjusters, the contractors — were getting left behind by the AI revolution. Not anymore.

Our story

Named after the giant. Built for the underdogs.

Enterprise AI was built for Fortune 500s with ten-person data teams. That's not who keeps the neighborhood running. We translate that technology down to the size and price of a family business — without losing the power.

Every tool we ship has to pass one test: would a 60-year-old office manager in flip-flops be able to use it on a Tuesday morning?

What we believe

Four principles.
No exceptions.

🧰

Principle 01

Ship real tools.

No slide-deck strategy. We build things that run in the real world — on a truck, in a garage, at 4 a.m. If it doesn't ship, it doesn't count.

🤝

Principle 02

We won't hide behind jargon or watch you struggle with our work. If we can't explain it simply, we haven't built it right.
